You are an expert Computer Vision Research Engineer and AI Systems Architect specializing in sports analytics, football analytics, multi-object tracking, video understanding, and production-grade ML pipelines.

I want to build a serious, research-oriented Computer Vision project for football/soccer that goes beyond basic object detection.

The project should take ordinary football broadcast footage as input and transform it into meaningful tactical or analytical information.

I do NOT want a project that is simply:

* Detect players with YOLO.
* Draw bounding boxes.
* Track objects.
* Display the result.

The goal is to build an end-to-end system where Computer Vision is used as the foundation for extracting higher-level football information.

## Project Requirements

The system should ideally be capable of:

1. Detecting relevant objects from broadcast footage:

   * Players
   * Goalkeepers
   * Referees
   * Ball
   * Other relevant objects when useful

2. Performing robust multi-object tracking across long videos.

3. Identifying and maintaining player identities/tracks despite:

   * Occlusion
   * Camera movement
   * Players leaving and re-entering the frame
   * Similar-looking players
   * Broadcast camera cuts
   * Replays
   * Zooms
   * Partial visibility

4. Classifying players by team using visual and/or spatial information.

5. Handling special cases correctly:

   * Goalkeepers
   * Referees
   * Substitutions
   * Players near the sidelines
   * Players temporarily occluded
   * Camera cuts

6. Recovering or estimating the football pitch geometry whenever possible.

7. Mapping detected players from image coordinates into a normalized 2D pitch coordinate system.

8. Using the reconstructed pitch-space information to derive higher-level football analytics.

## Most Important Requirement

I want the project to produce information that is useful from a football/tactical perspective.

Do not stop at detection and tracking.

Explore meaningful analytical layers such as:

* Team shape

For every proposed analytical feature, explicitly classify it as:

* Reliably measurable

* Not realistically reliable from ordinary broadcast footage

I want scientific honesty rather than impressive-looking but unreliable outputs.
